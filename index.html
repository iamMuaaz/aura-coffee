Our Story

Our Roots

From Soil to Soul

Every bag of Aura Coffee begins with a handshake — not a contract. We partner directly with small-hold farmers across Colombia, Ethiopia, and Guatemala, paying above fair-trade prices for ethically sourced, organically grown beans.

Each batch is small-batch roasted in-house to coax out the unique character of its origin, ensuring every cup tells the story of the soil, sun, and hands that made it possible.
